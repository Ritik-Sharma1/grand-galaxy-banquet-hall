A dedicated banquet venue in Kirti Nagar

Grand Galaxy Premium Banquet Hall hosts family celebrations and business functions at Tagore Market, Kirti Nagar — well connected to the Kirti Nagar metro corridor in West Delhi. The hall is set up to seat 50 to 200 guests, making it a practical fit for everything from intimate engagements to full wedding receptions.

Established in 2020, the venue is listed on Google with 269 reviews and on Justdial and Venuelook, where event planners can request pricing and check date availability.
